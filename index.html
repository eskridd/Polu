    What is remote viewing?

    Remote viewing is the practice of trying to describe a hidden target, such as a photo or a place, without any normal way of knowing what it is. It is done blind: you only have a target code, and you record whatever impressions come to mind.

    How to practice

    • Write quickly. First impressions are the point.
    • Stick to raw perceptions: colors, textures, shapes, sounds, motion, temperature.
    • Keep guesses separate. "Round and cold" is a perception; "a lake" is a guess.
    • Don't judge or edit as you go.
    • After the reveal, compare: note what you got and what you missed.

    Using Polu

    Each target has a code and nothing else. Write your impressions in Practice, press Reveal, and Polu shows the image beside your notes. Every practice is saved in your browser, so you can revisit it in History.
